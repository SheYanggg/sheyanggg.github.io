About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Materials Science and Engineering at Beijing Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Xinghua Yu.

I study how heat and stress develop during welding using finite-element simulation and physics-informed machine learning. My current work covers wire arc additive manufacturing, parallel thermal simulation, and in-process monitoring.
