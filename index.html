Computational welding · Scientific machine learning

Yang She

Ph.D. candidate, Beijing Institute of Technology

I study heat transfer and stress in welding using finite-element simulation and physics-informed machine learning. My research also covers parallel thermal simulation, magnetic-field-assisted welding, and in-process defect detection.

GitHub Google Scholar

Project experience

  • Physics-informed thermal modeling for wire arc additive manufacturing

    Doctoral research · Sep 2024–present

    Developed physics-informed models in PyTorch to predict transient temperatures during single-track wire arc deposition. Benchmarked the predictions against finite-element reference solutions.

  • Magnetic-field-assisted TIG welding

    Led the study · First-author publication

    Designed the methodology and curated the data for a study of how magnetic field orientation changes arc deflection, weld bead geometry, and grain size near the fusion line.

  • Multimodal defect detection in gas metal arc welding

    Software contributor · Second-author publication

    Contributed software to MBGN, which combines synchronized welding current and voltage signals with molten-pool images. The model achieved a macro-F1 of 0.719 on the held-out evaluation set.

  • Parallel finite-element simulation of welding heat transfer

    Benchmark contributor · Co-authored publication

    Contributed to multicore scaling tests and solver benchmarking for transient three-dimensional welding simulations. The team evaluated 17 Krylov solver and preconditioner combinations on 1–16 CPU cores using FEniCSx and PETSc.
